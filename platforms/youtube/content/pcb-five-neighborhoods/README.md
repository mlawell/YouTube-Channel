## ⚠️ The framing rule — read this before anything else

These are **Counts Real Estate listings**, and that collides head-on with the
strongest warning in the Day 5 material. Rachel Dau films 99.99% *other agents'*
listings on purpose:

> *"I never try and make something look like my own listing. I think that's
> awful. People could care less if your listing house is out there when they're
> relocating. They care what your heart is like and your values way more than if
> you're a listing agent in the area. So the less you talk about yourself and
> your production, probably the better."* — `D5-FS` 00:33:08–00:36:33

**So this is not "five Counts listings." It is "five Panama City Beach
neighborhoods and what living in each one is actually like."** The homes
illustrate the neighborhoods. They are not the product.

Four things enforce that, and they are load-bearing:

1. **No price and no "for sale" in the title or thumbnail.** A named rule
   (`D1-QA` 00:15:37). See [`metadata.md`](metadata.md) and
   [`thumbnail-brief.md`](thumbnail-brief.md).
2. **Roughly 60% neighborhood, 40% home** in every segment, so the video
   survives the listings selling. The script is budgeted that way explicitly —
   about 3 minutes of place before about 2 minutes of house.
3. **The word "listing" is never spoken.** Neither is "my listing," "we have it
   listed," or any variation. The homes are examples of what money buys in that
   neighborhood.
4. **No claim that any of these will still be available.** They will not be.
   Every one is stated as *"as I'm recording this"*, the same convention the
   phases video uses for live inventory.

### Why these five and not the five that were proposed

Three of the proposed five survived. Two did not, and the reasons are factual
rather than editorial.

**✅ Kept: Bay Point (1722 Wahoo Cir).** Unimprovable. The MLS Community Features
field literally reads `BasketballCourt,Fishing,Golf,Marina,Playground,TennisCourts,Gated`,
the description confirms a Jack Nicklaus course, a full-service marina, 24-hour
security and an on-site restaurant, and the HOA is a checkable $1,650/year. It is
the strongest "what living there is like" segment available.

**✅ Kept: Palmetto Trace (249 Oxford Ave)** — and promoted to segment one.
The MLS Directions field says the front entrance is **"located across the street
From Pier Park."** That is a verified anchor-point adjacency written by the
listing agent, and Pier Park is where the video starts. It also has the lowest
tax bill in the package by an order of magnitude: **$384 a year.**

**⚠️ Kept but completely reframed: Bahama Beach (13003 Oleander Dr).** The plan
described this as a *"classic beach cottage, deepest photo set."* It is not a
cottage. It is a **$5,299,000, seven-bedroom, 5,500 sq ft, brand-new 2025
Gulf-front house with a heated second-floor pool, an elevator, a private
boardwalk to the sand, and seven weeks of rental already pre-booked.** It is the
only genuinely Gulf-front property in the entire set. Reframed, it is *better*
than the plan assumed: it is the "buy the beach and rent it when you're not
here" life, which is a real and specific Panama City Beach option most relocating
buyers do not know is available to them.

**❌ Dropped: the "beachfront condo" slot — because there is no condo.** The plan
offered Royal Palms of Laguna Beach *or* Bare Footin Condominium. **Both are
`Single Family` in the MLS Type field**, despite one being named
"Condominium." Royal Palms is a 5-bed, 5-bath, 2,783 sq ft house; Bare Footin is
a 6-bed, 3,720 sq ft house. There is **no beachfront condo in this media set at
all**, so the product-type slot cannot be filled honestly and was replaced.

**❌ Dropped: The Glades (124 Hombre Cir) — golf duplicates Bay Point.** Bay Point
has a Jack Nicklaus course *and* a marina *and* a guarded gate. Running a second
golf segment spends five minutes to say something the video has already said
better. The Glades also has **the weakest gallery in the package (34 photos)**,
which makes its tour file the shortest at 1:45.

**➕ Added: El Centro Beach (263 Lullwater Dr) — the genuinely surprising one.**
MLS `Waterfront = true`, but `View = Lake,Water`. It is **lakefront, not
Gulf-front**, on **0.66 acres** — nearly three times the median lot in the
package — for $890,000. "You can have water and land and quiet without paying
Gulf-front money" is a real option nobody making PCB content covers, and it
breaks up an otherwise beach-heavy video.

**➕ Added: Treasure Cove (2601 Oak St) — the affordability answer, and the
closer.** At **$365,000** it is the cheapest way into Panama City Beach in the
whole set, it has the **second-deepest gallery (152 photos)**, and it sits on
**0.36 acres — more land than the $5.3 million house on 0.17.** Ending the video
here means the last thing a viewer hears is a number that makes the town feel
possible.

**The result: a $365,000 to $5,299,000 spread across one video, with no two
segments answering the same question.**

## 🔴 The finding that changes the plan: the virtual tours are slideshows

The plan's core technical bet was this: Karen narrates through HeyGen and
ElevenLabs, which puts her in the framework's weak case — *"voiceover-over-B-roll
rarely clears 10 min AVD; a live filmed walk-through does"* (`D1-QA` 00:48:32),
and an AI avatar *"cannot replicate a vlog or a walk-through home tour."* The
mitigation was supposed to be the fifteen ~2-minute virtual tours: five of them
would give roughly ten minutes of genuine motion footage.

**They are not filmed footage.** They are auto-generated Ken Burns slideshows
built from the same gallery JPEGs sitting next to them in the folder. Measured:

- **13 of the 15 are 123.1 seconds to the frame.** The only two that are shorter
  are the two smallest galleries.
- The fit is exact: **2.84 seconds per photo plus 9.5 seconds of cards, capped at
  40 photos.** 34 photos → 105.7 s. 37 photos → 114.4 s. Every one of the 15 sits
  on that line.
- Sampled video frames match specific gallery JPEGs at **0.99 correlation**.

So the mitigation does not exist. Using the tour files changes nothing about the
AVD risk, because they are exactly the thing the framework warns about — stills
with movement painted on — just pre-packaged. Full measurements in
[`media-map.md`](media-map.md#-read-this-before-you-cut-anything-what-the-virtual-tour-files-actually-are).

**Three things follow, and they are already built into the script.**

**1. The mitigation is the Pier Park drone footage instead — and it is better.**
`PCB\Pier Park\` holds **21 minutes and 23 seconds of 4K 60 fps aerial
footage** across seven clips, roughly 13 GB, plus four 4K stills. This is real,
moving, genuinely-shot material and it was not in the plan. The script uses it in
the cold open, in **all five neighborhood transitions**, under the Palmetto Trace
segment, and under the close.

**2. The tour files stop being the spine and become one ingredient.** After
trimming the cards, five of them give **9 minutes 28 seconds** against a
30-minute video — under a third of the runtime. The script budgets them at
roughly 90 seconds per segment and fills the rest with Karen, aerials, and
hand-cut gallery stills.

**3. There are 276 gallery photos the tour files never touch.** Because the
generator caps at 40, **Bahama Beach's 172-photo gallery yields 132 unused
photos** and **Treasure Cove's 152-photo gallery yields 112.** The two richest
sets in the package are the two the tour files use the smallest fraction of.
Segments 2 and 5 are built from the gallery by hand for exactly this reason.

### 🔴 And the tour files have the price burned into frame one

The first 4.1 seconds of every tour file is a title card reading the address, the
city, **and the price**. The last 5.4 seconds is Karen's Counts-branded outro
card.

This directly attacks the video's main structural mechanic. The whole design
holds each home's number to the **end** of its segment — five micro-reveals, one
per neighborhood, following the band-orients / number-pays-off finding. **Drop a
tour file in whole and the price is on screen before Karen has said a word about
the neighborhood.**

```
Trim IN at 0:04.5    Trim OUT at 1:57.5
```

That is in [`media-map.md`](media-map.md#-the-trim-rule--non-negotiable) as a
non-negotiable, and it is repeated at every tour cue in the script. There is a
second reason to obey it: **these prices move.** Bay Point has been cut five
times and Treasure Cove four. A burned-in number is stale the day it changes and
stale permanently, because it is baked into the pixels.

## Sourcing: two tiers of data in one file

⚠️ **Read this before building any future video from these detail sheets. It is
the trap in this data and it is not obvious.**

Each `listing-details.md` contains **two kinds of information with completely
different reliability**, and they sit inches apart in the same file.

| Tier | What it is | Where it lives | Treat as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — MLS structured fields** | Price, DOM, price history, beds, baths, sq ft, lot size, year built, assigned schools, HOA status/fee/frequency, annual tax, community features, waterfront and view flags, security features | The `\| Field \| Value \|` tables | ✅ **Citable.** Entered under MLS rules and subject to correction |
| **2 — The listing prose** | Everything in `## Property description` | One free-text block | 🔴 **Unverified marketing copy**, written to sell a house |

**Tier 2 is where the errors are**, and it is also where the most quotable
material is, which is exactly what makes it dangerous.

### The correction that proved it

The marketing copy for 249 Oxford Ave states:

> *"Tallahassee Memorial Hospital (coming soon), A major medical facility
> arriving nearby, bringing long-term value and stability to the surrounding
> area."*

**It is not Tallahassee Memorial.** The hospital being built on Bay Parkway is
**FSU Health Panama City Beach** — corrected by Mike Lawell, who lives here. And
Karen's own library holds **2 minutes 25 seconds of 4K footage of it** at
`PCB\West Bay & HWY 79 Corridor\FSU Health Panama City Beach\FSU Health Panama City Beach 01.MP4`.

An earlier draft of this package carried the TMH claim into the script on the
strength of "it's in the detail sheet." **The detail sheet was never the
authority — the MLS fields in it were.** That single sentence also contains a
factual error, an unverifiable drive-time implication, and a forward-looking
value claim a licensee should not state flatly. One sentence, three problems.

### What tier 2 typically contains, and how this script handles each

| Prose claim type | Example from these files | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Named third parties** | "Tallahassee Memorial Hospital" | 🔴 **Wrong.** Corrected to FSU Health, delivered over Karen's own footage |
| **Drive times / "minutes away"** | "ECP, Only 15 minutes away"; "approximately a 10-minute drive to the nearest public beach access" | **All marked `[KAREN — verify]`.** Never spoken as fact |
| **Superlatives** | "one of Panama City Beach's most coveted neighborhoods"; "world-class"; "premier"; "iconic" | **None used.** Sales language, and not Karen's assessment to make |
| **Scarcity / investment claims** | "homes here don't last long"; "bringing long-term value and stability"; "INSTANT EQUITY" | **None repeated as Karen's own.** Forward-looking value claims a licensee should avoid stating flatly. The Treasure Cove segment **rebuts** its "instant equity" line using MLS price history |
| **Condition and system claims** | 2019 gut renovation, impact glass, generator, "brand-new roof (2026)" | **Marked.** Insurance-relevant and should not rest on a seller's word |
| **Seller's own business figures** | "7 weeks of pre-bookings" | **Marked.** Persuasive, unverifiable, and exactly what a buyer would rely on |
| **Amenity attributions** | "Jack Nicklaus-designed", "full-service marina" | **Marked.** MLS confirms *golf* and *marina*; the prose says *whose* and *how good* |
| **Landmark names** | Pier Park, Frank Brown Park, Aaron Bessant Park, Russell Fields Pier | **Kept, flagged.** Almost certainly fine and good material |
| **Exclusivity claims** | "PCB's only dog-friendly beach section" | **Marked.** The script gives a fallback line that drops "only" and cannot be wrong |

### One genuine grey area, stated honestly

The MLS **`Directions`** field is a *structured field containing agent-authored
free text*. This package treats it as **tier 1 for the route and tier 2 for any
distance** — so *"across the street from Pier Park"* and the turn-by-turn
sequences are cited as **verified routes**, while no minute figure is ever taken
from them. That is why every entry in
[the drive-time table](#drive-time-table--karen-all-of-it) is `[KAREN]` and every
route beside it is not.

### The rule going forward

> **MLS structured fields are citable. Listing prose is a lead, not a source.**
> Anything from the description block gets confirmed independently or gets marked
> `[KAREN — verify]`. It never enters a script as a stated fact, however good it
> sounds — and it will often sound very good, because someone was paid to make it
> sound that way.

> ### 🔗 The same failure mode arrives by a second route
> **The artifact was real; the inference drawn from it was not.** The channel has
> now hit this twice:
>
> | Route | Artifact | Bad inference |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | **Listing prose** | A real MLS detail sheet | *"Tallahassee Memorial Hospital (coming soon)"* |
> | **Aerial footage** | Real drone video of the same hospital | *"the bay is visible"* — true of a drone at altitude, false from the ground |
>
> The companion rule is **"drone footage shows what a drone sees, never narrate
> it as what a resident sees"** in
> [`karen-presenter-treatment.md`](../karen-presenter-treatment.md#-drone-footage-shows-what-a-drone-sees).
> Both were caught by the Lawells rather than by any file. **A source being
> genuine says nothing about whether a particular claim drawn from it is true.**

**Karen is the correction mechanism here, and she is a better one than the
data.** Both fixes to this package came from the Lawells' own knowledge of the
area, not from any file. That is the actual competitive advantage this channel
has over [Mr. Beach](../../../../knowledge/competitors/mrbeach.md), whose
authority is personal and anecdotal but unfalsifiable — and it only works if the
script asks her the right questions instead of assuming the paperwork is right.

## Runtime: why 28–32 minutes

**The case for it.** Average view duration must clear **7 minutes absolute**
(Limiter #2). At 30 minutes that needs **23%** retention; at 15 minutes it needs
**47%**. And 28–32 sits inside what this market actually publishes — every
real-estate channel measured in
[`framework-vs-practice.md`](../../../../knowledge/reference-videos/framework-vs-practice.md#-finding-2-1828-minutes-is-the-floor-not-the-target)
has a median at or above 18:49, the founders' own channels sit at 25:41 and
35:48, and **Mr. Beach's PCB content runs 43:43, 30:51 and 32:35.**

**The case against it, stated honestly.** *"There's no such thing as a too long
video. There is such thing as a too long, unengaging video"* — **but Jesse adds
"not on a brand-new channel, no trust built yet"** (`D2-QA` 00:05:37). This
channel has one video and 54 views on it. That caveat is aimed precisely at
Karen's situation and it should not be waved away.

**Why 28–32 anyway.** Five distinct segments with five different homes, five
price reveals and five neighborhood changes is a fundamentally different
retention proposition from thirty minutes of one map. The video has five natural
re-entry points, and chapters let a viewer jump to the neighborhood they care
about — which still accumulates watch time. Cutting to 15 minutes would mean
three minutes per neighborhood, which is not enough to do the 60/40 split that
[the framing rule](#-the-framing-rule--read-this-before-anything-else) requires.

> ⚠️ **Watch the first retention release.** If the graph sags inside segments
> rather than between them, the segments are too long — cut to four
> neighborhoods at 5 minutes on the next one rather than shortening all five.
> If it sags *between* segments, the re-hooks are not landing and the transitions
> need work. Those are different failures with different fixes.

## Structure

The [vlog-tour method](../../../../knowledge/channel-junkies/playbook/key-points/win-with-youtube-challenge.md)
(`D4-GS` 00:49:29–00:51:24): open at an anchor location, state the itinerary up
front, drive each one covering what it is like to live there and how far it is,
and **be real, not promotional**.

| Block | Time | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open — call-out, hook, price band | 0:00–0:22 | 0:22 |
| Who Karen is, and the itinerary | 0:22–1:40 | 1:18 |
| The re-hooks, planted | 1:40–2:15 | 0:35 |
| **Pier Park** — the anchor, and the drive-time spine | 2:15–3:40 | 1:25 |
| **1 · Palmetto Trace** | 3:40–8:50 | 5:10 |
| **2 · Bahama Beach** | 8:50–14:20 | 5:30 |
| **3 · El Centro Beach** | 14:20–19:20 | 5:00 |
| **4 · Bay Point** | 19:20–24:50 | 5:30 |
| The schools answer — re-hook payoff | 24:50–25:40 | 0:50 |
| **5 · Treasure Cove** | 25:40–29:35 | 3:55 |
| Redirect, then dead air | 29:35–30:00 | 0:25 |

Each of the five segments runs the same five beats: **where it is and how far ·
what living there is like and who it suits, plus one honest drawback · things to
do nearby · the home · the price.**

**The eight characteristics**, applied:

- **Call-out** inside 4 seconds, driver city then step down — *"So you're
  thinking about moving to Panama City Beach, Florida."*
- **Hook** stacked immediately, with a **price band** (`$365,000 to $5.3 million`)
  as part of it. This is the framework's own affordability exception and the
  band-orients finding: bands land at a median of 14% through the best videos in
  the catalog, numbers at 86%.
- **Re-hooks** — three, planted before 2:15 and paid off at 24:50, 25:40 and
  29:20. This is the main retention mechanism in a five-part video and it is
  planted deliberately, not hoped for.
- **One CTA at a time.** First spoken CTA at **4:15**, inside segment 1. Hard ask
  at 29:05, immediately before the final number.
- **Five micro-reveals** — each home's number at the end of its own segment, at
  roughly 27%, 47%, 64%, 82% and 97% of runtime.
- **Redirect last**, after the final price, naming exactly one next video.
  ⚠️ `channel-setup-config.md` still shows *"Add end screens to videos"*
  unchecked — the spoken redirect is doing this job alone until that is fixed.
- **The last 20 seconds carry no facts.**

exist yet.** Branch `mlawell-karen-voice-and-humor` was checked and currently

  Every intended laugh is on the page.

  and YouTube throttles content indexing high on AI detection (`D1-QA`
  00:03:34). The script uses commas and full stops in all spoken copy. *Prose in
  these markdown files is not read aloud and is not subject to the rule.*
- **Mike** appears twice, lightly, both marked `[KAREN — MIKE]` for approval.
  He is being developed as a recurring character; this video is not the place to
  launch him properly.

## Things not to say on camera

- **Any drive time that has not been driven.** See the table above. Several were
  lifted from listing copy in an earlier draft and are now all `[KAREN]`.
- 🔴 **"Tallahassee Memorial."** The hospital on Bay Parkway is **FSU Health
  Panama City Beach.** The listing copy is wrong and Karen has footage of the
  real one.
- **Anything read straight out of a listing description.** See
  [Sourcing](#sourcing-two-tiers-of-data-in-one-file). It is marketing copy, not
  data.
- **"Most coveted", "homes here don't last long", "long-term value and
  stability", "instant equity."** All are from these listings' copy. They are
  someone else's sales language, and the value claims are the kind a licensee
  should not state flatly.
- **"This home is for sale" / "my listing" / "we have it listed."** See
  [the framing rule](#-the-framing-rule--read-this-before-anything-else).
- **Any price without "as I'm recording this."** Four of the five have moved.
- **"Condo."** There is no condo in this video. Bare Footin and Royal Palms are
  both `Single Family` in the MLS despite the names.
- **Any flood-zone, insurance-cost, or HOA-rule claim not in a detail sheet.**
  Flood zone in particular is not in the MLS data, it is the single most
  consequential thing a PCB buyer gets wrong, and guessing it on camera is the
  worst available outcome. If it comes up: *"that's a per-address question and I
  will pull the actual flood determination for you."*
- **Any school quality claim.** The *assignments* are verified from the MLS.
  Ratings, test scores and reputations are not, and they change.
- **Any claim about what Bay Point's HOA covers** beyond the two things the
  detail sheet lists (`Playground,Security`).
- 🔴 **"Gulf of Mexico" / "Gulf of America."** ⛔ **Karen says "the Gulf."**
  It is what locals say, both formal names are currently politically loaded in
  opposite directions, and the training is explicit about keeping charged terms
  off a relocation channel (`D4-GS` 00:12:35). This package is where it matters
  most, since the Gulf is central to the pitch rather than a distance. Channel-wide
  rule: [`karen-voice-and-humor.md`](../karen-voice-and-humor.md).
  **"Gulf-front" is fine** — a standard real-estate compound, not the water's
  name — and **"HCA FL Gulf Coast" is a hospital's proper name**, never edited.
- **Another party's appraisal as a fact.** Attribute it or drop it.
