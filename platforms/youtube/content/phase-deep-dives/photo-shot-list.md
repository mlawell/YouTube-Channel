# Photo shot list — Phase Deep Dives

**What Karen shoots, per phase, and which beat each photo is covering.**

This is a golf-cart afternoon, not a production. Every shot on this list is
reachable from a cart and takes about ninety seconds.

---

## Why this matters more than it looks

The framework is blunt about the weakness of this format. Voiceover over B-roll
**rarely clears 10 minutes of average view duration**, while a live filmed
walk-through does (`D1-QA` 00:48:32), and an AI avatar *"cannot replicate a vlog
or a walk-through home tour."* An avatar over a static map frame is the weakest
visual this channel has, and on the deep dives it is most of the runtime.

**Photographs Karen took herself are proof of presence.** They are the closest
this format gets to demonstration, and they are the one thing a competitor
working from Minto renders cannot fake. Mr. Beach has to borrow a resident to get
this ([`mrbeach.md`](../../../../knowledge/competitors/mrbeach.md)). Karen just walks
outside.

Rachel Dau's rule for low-visual stretches is *"there's not much to see, so this
needs to be much to say"* (`D5-FS` 00:24:18). A photo changes the arithmetic: now
there is something to see, and the talking gets easier.

## ⭐ Street signs are the priority shot

Street-name signs are the cheapest high-value photograph in this community:
trivially shootable from a cart, unmistakably local, and they carry the running
Buffett gag that is Karen's signature detail.

**Shoot the confirmed ones.** Verified list:
[`street-names-buffett.md`](../../../../properties/latitude-margaritaville-watersound/street-names-buffett.md).
A sign photographed for a joke that turns out not to be a real reference is a
correction in the comments, so shoot from the confirmed table, not from memory.

## Rules

- **Karen's own photographs only.** The whole value is that she took them. A Minto render or a stock beach shot destroys the argument the photo was there to make.
- **Shoot signs straight on and legible.** A street sign nobody can read is a wasted shot and a wasted joke.
- **No people without permission**, and no house that identifies a neighbour's home without asking. Several thousand neighbours may watch these.
- **Date the concert-night shots.** If a Bandshell photo gets reused across ten episodes that is fine, but Karen should know which night it was if anyone asks.
- **Nothing here overrides a `[KAREN]` gate.** A photo of a street is not a cart time and is not resident feedback.

## Where these land in the scripts

Photos slot into the existing `[B-ROLL]` and `[FRAME]` cues in
`phase-1.md` … `phase-10.md`. Those scripts are generated by
`tools/scripts/build_phase_scripts.py` — to add a permanent cue, edit the
generator, not the markdown.
