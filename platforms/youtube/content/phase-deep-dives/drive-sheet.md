# Drive sheet — cart times and resident feedback

Generated by `tools/scripts/build_phase_scripts.py`. **This is the only source for the two things in the phase videos that are not public record.** Fill it in, then re-read the drafts.

## How to run it

1. Start at the phase, end at the Town Center. Same end point every time, or the numbers are not comparable.
2. Hold 30 mph where it is safe and legal — that is the number quoted on screen. If a phase cannot be driven at 30, write down what it can be driven at; that is itself worth saying.
3. Film it. A cart-POV run with a visible timer is the proof, and it is the B-roll.
4. **A measured time below the floor is impossible.** The floor is the straight line. If the stopwatch beats it, something is wrong — wrong start point, wrong end point, or a mistyped number.
