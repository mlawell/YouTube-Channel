# TikTok

**Status: Not started.** Short-form vertical video for reach and discovery.

## Getting started

- Cut hooks and highlights from long-form YouTube tours
  ([`../../tools/video`](../../tools/video)) into 15–60s vertical clips.
- Lead with a search-style question or bold hook (see the title/hook guidance in
  [`../../knowledge/channel-junkies/playbook/realtor-playbook.md`](../../knowledge/channel-junkies/playbook/realtor-playbook.md)).
- Apply brand identity from [`../../brand/README.md`](../../brand/README.md).

## Content & assets

- `content/` — hooks, short scripts, caption + hashtag sets.
- `assets/` — vertical clips, cover frames.
