# Instagram

**Status: Not started.** Visual-first platform for Reels, carousels, and Stories.

## Getting started

- Repurpose vertical clips and highlights from long-form YouTube tours (produced
  with [`../../tools/video`](../../tools/video)).
- Apply brand identity from [`../../brand/README.md`](../../brand/README.md).
- Reuse relocation/lifestyle angles from the
  [`../../knowledge/`](../../knowledge/README.md) library.

## Content & assets

- `content/` — Reel scripts, caption copy, hashtag sets, carousel outlines.
- `assets/` — vertical clips, cover frames, story templates, profile art.
