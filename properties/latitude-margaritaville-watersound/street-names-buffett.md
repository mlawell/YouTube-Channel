# Street names and the Jimmy Buffett catalogue

*Verified 2026-08-23. Street names and phase attribution from
[`streets_by_phase.md`](streets_by_phase.md), which is built from Bay County
public records. Song, album and brand attributions are researched separately and
carry their own confidence rating.*

Latitude Margaritaville Watersound is a Minto community built under licence from
Margaritaville Holdings, so deliberate Buffett references in the street names are
expected rather than fanciful. The proof that it is deliberate is the pair:
**Latitude Blvd** and **Attitude Ave**, from *Changes in Latitudes, Changes in
Attitudes*.

But not every street is a reference, and **three of the ones most often claimed
as references are not Buffett at all.** This page exists so nobody says one of
those on camera.

> ⚠️ **Read this before scripting a joke about a street name.**
> Parrotheads know this catalogue far better than we do. A wrong attribution
> gets corrected in the comments, and the correction costs more credibility than
> the joke ever earned. Only the **confirmed** rows below are safe to state as
> fact. Everything else is either "fits the theme" or silence.

## ⚠️ The three traps

| Street | The claim you'll hear | What it actually is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No Shoes Ct** | *"No Shoes, No Shirt, No Problems"* | **Kenny Chesney**, 2002. Not Buffett. He never wrote or recorded it. Saying this on camera in a Margaritaville community is the single most correctable error on this page |
| **Cool Water Way** | a Buffett song called *"Cool Water"* | No confirmed Buffett recording. *"Cool Water"* was written by **Bob Nolan in 1936** and first recorded by the **Sons of the Pioneers** in 1941. **This is Karen and Mike's own street** |
| **Escape Ave** | a Buffett song called *"Escape"* | There is no such song. *Escape to Margaritaville* is a 2017 jukebox musical, which is a brand reference at best |

Two more worth being careful with:

- **Carnival Ln** — *Don't Stop the Carnival* (1998) is a real Buffett concept album, from the Herman Wouk novel. But there is no song called "Carnival," and one word off an album title is thin. Listed below as **theme**. If Karen wants it, cite the album, never a song.
- **Coral Reef Way** — the **Coral Ree**f**er** Band drops a syllable. The reference is almost certainly intended, but the spelling is not identical and it is a band, not a song.

## UNVERIFIED

None. Every one of the 60 street names in county record resolved into one of the
three categories above.

**What could not be established:** whether Minto documented any of this
deliberately. No official Minto or Margaritaville Holdings list of street-name
references exists in public. There is AI-generated fan content online that
asserts references for **Good Life Way** and **Beach Bum Blvd** in particular;
none of it could be corroborated against the actual discography and it should not
be used. Our attributions are inference from the catalogue, not confirmation from
the builder, and the confirmed rows are the ones where that inference is safe.

## ⭐ The density trend: tested, and it does not hold

The appealing story is that Buffett density climbs as the phases go on: Phase 1
is barely a reference, Phase 10 is wall-to-wall. **It is not true, and it should
not be said on camera.**

Counted against the streets each video actually names — the ones with a county
address range or an addressed parcel, which is what the script generator puts on
screen:

| Phase | Confirmed | Theme | Not a ref | Streets named | Confirmed rate |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 3 | 1 | 0 | 4 | 75% |
| **3** | **7** | 3 | 1 | 11 | 64% |
| 4 | 4 | 6 | 0 | 10 | 40% |
| 5 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 4 | 50% |
| 6 | 4 | 4 | 0 | 8 | 50% |
| 7 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 5 | 60% |
| **8** | **0** | 3 | 0 | 3 | **0%** |
| 9 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 9 | 44% |
| 10 | 5 | 3 | 0 | 8 | 62% |

Confirmed references by phase, 1 through 10: **1, 3, 7, 4, 2, 4, 3, 0, 4, 5.**

That does not climb. It does not climb in absolute count and it does not climb as
a rate. The correlation between phase number and confirmed-reference count is
**r = +0.064**, which is nothing. **Phase 3 is the densest phase in the community
at 7, not Phase 10 at 5.**

### The two facts that are true, and are better jokes anyway

**1. Phase 8 — Karen's own — is the only phase in the entire community with zero
confirmed Buffett references.** Cool Water Way, Escape Avenue, Hang Loose Court.
All three fit the theme, none of the three is traceable to a song. **Karen and
Mike live on Cool Water Way**, which sounds like a Buffett song and is a 1936
Bob Nolan cowboy song instead. Out of sixteen recorded plats, the realtor who
lives here and makes the videos about it bought on the one street set with no
Jimmy Buffett in it. That is verified, it is funny, and it is at her own expense,
which is the only kind of joke that costs nothing.

**2. Phase 1 is 100% Buffett** — because the one street in it that carries county
house numbers is Margaritaville Boulevard itself. Highway 79 touches the plat but
has no addresses in it, so it isn't a street in the neighbourhood. The first plat
on the books is one street long and that street is the name of the song.

**3. Phase 3 is the densest at 7** — and Phase 3 is also the phase people can't
agree is one phase. The phase that is really three plats is also the one with the
most Buffett in it.

*Counts are reproducible: they use the same plat grouping and the same
"streets worth naming" filter as `tools/scripts/build_phase_scripts.py`.*
