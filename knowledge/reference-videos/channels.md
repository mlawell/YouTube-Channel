# Channels

The channels behind [`catalog.md`](catalog.md) — who they are, what they publish,
and what each one is worth to Karen.

**Subscriber counts** come from `exemplars.json` (captured 2026-08-21). **Median
runtime**, **≥18 min** and **median views** are computed over the most recent
45–60 videos per channel from the channel listing files. Listing view counts are
**rounded by YouTube**, so medians of views are approximate; durations are exact.

## Channel Junkies — the coaching channel

`https://www.youtube.com/@ChannelJunkies` · **~20,800 subscribers**

Free long-form versions of the paid material. **Instructional, not exemplary.**

**Median runtime 12:38. Median views 241.** Against ~20,800 subscribers, that is
roughly 1% of the subscriber base watching a given video — by far the weakest
ratio in this catalog. Its two best-performing videos are also its two longest
(53:08 → 670 views; 58:23 → 569).

That is not a criticism of the teaching. A coaching channel sells to a small
professional audience and is not competing for relocation intent, so its metrics
mean something different. But it does mean **Karen should watch this channel and
model nothing on it.** Video list: [`catalog.md`](catalog.md#instructional-videos--watch-these-dont-copy-them).

| Living in New Smyrna Beach FL | 2,950 | [Margaritaville Daytona Beach](https://youtu.be/cjbjj_TSqeQ) | 13:20 | 6,010 |

**Read these with care.** They are the three shortest videos in the catalog and
two of the three highest-viewed, which looks like an argument against Finding 2.
It probably isn't: The Villages video is essentially **tourism content** for the
most-searched retirement destination in America, and neither it nor the
Explore55Plus roundup is trying to generate relocation leads in a specific
market. High views on broad-interest content is a different business from
7 minutes of absolute watch time on buyer-intent content. Worth understanding;
not worth copying.
