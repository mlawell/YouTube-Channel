# Reference Video Catalog

Real videos from working relocation realtors, measured against the Channel
Junkies framework. Grouped **by format**, because format — not channel — is
what decides a video's structure.

Read [`README.md`](README.md) first for the ground rules and the caption
caveat. Machine-readable version: [`catalog.json`](catalog.json).

## How to read the numbers

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Runtime / Views** | Exact, from yt-dlp metadata captured 2026-08-21 |
| **1st price** | The first credible mention of a *real-estate* price, hand-verified against the surrounding sentence |
| **%** | Where that lands as a share of runtime |
| **Kind** | `band` = a range used to orient ("the 350s", "400k to 1.5M"). `number` = a specific figure attached to a specific home |
| **Reveal** | The closing price reveal, where the video has one |

> **The band/number distinction is the most important thing in this catalog.**
> It is what reconciles the framework's "never reveal price until the end"
> with the fact that almost every successful community video gives a price in
> the first two minutes. See
> [`framework-vs-practice.md`](framework-vs-practice.md#-finding-1-the-price-rule-is-about-numbers-not-bands).
