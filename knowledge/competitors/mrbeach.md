## What he does well — take these

1. **He publishes long, and it works.** Median 27:38 across his last 30 videos;
   **27 of 30** are 18 minutes or longer; his Latitude tours run 42 and 46
   minutes. His single best video, at 50,030 views, is 30:51. Length is not
   costing him anything.
2. **A resident co-host does the credibility work.** Rick is introduced as *"my
   good friend Rick. Rick knows everything about Margaritaville"* and described
   in the video description as a local who actually lives in the community. The
   format is two neighbours walking and talking, not an interview with a sales
   rep.
3. **Anti-sales positioning.** His descriptions run *"I'm not here to sell you a
   house. I'm here to make sure you don't get burned."* His title pattern is
   consistently disclosure-framed — *What They Don't Tell You*, *The Truth
   About…*, *Why People REGRET…*, *10 People Who Should NOT Move…* This is the
   single most copyable thing about the channel.
4. **He orients on price immediately.** In the model tour, *"these start in the
   350s"* lands at **1:38** of a 46-minute video. He does not make the viewer
   wait to find out whether the community is affordable.
5. **A concrete, repeated offer.** A **$777 closing bonus** for buyers who use
   him, stated on camera and repeated at the close. Whatever one thinks of it,
   it is specific and memorable, which most agent CTAs are not.
6. **He references his own prior videos on camera** — *"our last video did so
   well, we had over 12,000 people watch"* — which is a redirect and social
   proof in one line.

## Where the gaps are

**1. ⭐ He has no phases video.** Three Latitude videos, a regional map video,
and **nothing that answers "which phase is which."** The strongest channel in
this market has left Karen's exact topic unanswered. This is the gap, and it is
larger than the one @TheLatitudeGuy leaves.

**2. He rents the residency Karen owns.** His entire authority on Latitude runs
through Rick, because Mr. Beach does not live there. Karen and her husband live
in **Phase 8**. That is the one asset in this market that cannot be borrowed,
hired or copied — and he has demonstrated exactly how valuable it is by building
two videos around someone else's.

**3. No chapters, on 40-plus-minute videos.** Neither Latitude tour has them.
On a 46-minute model walkthrough that is a real usability failure — a viewer who
wants the Dreamsicle cottage has to scrub blind. Karen's per-phase chapters are a
straightforward advantage.

**4. Models and amenities, not the buying decision.** He covers what the homes
look like and what the community feels like. He does not cover **which phases
are resale-only**, where the Highway 79 noise falls, how far the Bandshell
carries, or where the community actually ends. Those are the facts that decide a
purchase, and they are the spine of Karen's script.

**5. No public-record sourcing.** His authority is personal and anecdotal —
which works well, but it means nothing he says is checkable. Karen's map is
generated from Bay County recorded plats with book and page on screen.

co-hosts is authority transfer to a *salesperson*, which Rick is not.

## ⚠️ What this changes about Karen's plan

**Runtime is the live issue.** Karen's phases script targets 19–22 minutes
against a direct competitor publishing **42:03 and 46:11 on the same
community** — roughly half. The 19–22 figure was set to clear the 7-minute
average-view-duration limiter, and it does. But it was never set against what
this market actually rewards, and this channel is the evidence that the market
rewards more. See
[`platforms/youtube/content/latitude-phases-explained/README.md`](../../platforms/youtube/content/latitude-phases-explained/README.md).

**The co-host question is worth reopening.** Channel Junkies says don't. The
best-performing videos on Karen's exact community both do. Karen's husband is a
Phase 8 resident, which is a stronger version of what Rick provides.
