# Competitors

Short analyses of other agents and channels working the same markets Karen
works. Each note covers what the competitor does, what they do well worth
copying, and the concrete gaps we can exploit in our own content.

## Rules

- **Summarize, never republish.** No verbatim transcripts, descriptions or
  copied assets. Link to the source and describe it.
- **Verify claims before calling them wrong.** Every factual correction in these
  notes cites a public record we actually checked. A wrong "correction" costs
  more credibility than saying nothing.
- **Stay useful, not petty.** These notes exist to sharpen our own content
  briefs. Nothing here should end up on camera as an attack on a named person —
  correct the claim, not the human.
